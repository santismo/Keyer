How to use

Choose a scale or mode, then drag a wheel center or use the key buttons. Every formula remains fixed while the note names transpose. Side A emphasizes major-key degree relationships. Side B emphasizes modes, minor scales, pentatonics, blues, and symmetrical scales.
Recreated as an original interactive study tool based on the visible two-sided wheel concept, not a copy of the printed artwork or book text.
